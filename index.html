1. Axes, Planes & Point Positions

Coordinate Planes

  • • $XY$-plane: $z = 0$, point format $(x, y, 0)$
  • • $YZ$-plane: $x = 0$, point format $(0, y, z)$
  • • $ZX$-plane: $y = 0$, point format $(x, 0, z)$

Points on Axes

  • • On $X$-axis: $(x, 0, 0)$
  • • On $Y$-axis: $(0, y, 0)$
  • • On $Z$-axis: $(0, 0, z)$
  • • Origin $O$: $(0, 0, 0)$

2. Distances in 3D Space

Distance between $P(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ and $Q(x_2,y_2,z_2)$:

$$PQ = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2 + (z_2-z_1)^2}$$

Distance of $P(x,y,z)$ from Axes:

• From $X$-axis: $\sqrt{y^2 + z^2}$

• From $Y$-axis: $\sqrt{x^2 + z^2}$

• From $Z$-axis: $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$

Distance of $P(x,y,z)$ from Planes:

• From $XY$-plane: $|z|$

• From $YZ$-plane: $|x|$

• From $ZX$-plane: $|y|$

3. Section Formulae & Centroids

Section Division ($m:n$)

Dividing line joining $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ & $(x_2,y_2,z_2)$

Internal: $\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n}, \frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}, \frac{mz_2+nz_1}{m+n}\right)$

External: $\left(\frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n}, \frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n}, \frac{mz_2-nz_1}{m-n}\right)$

Centroids

Triangle Centroid:
$G = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3}, \frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3}, \frac{z_1+z_2+z_3}{3}\right)$

Tetrahedron Centroid:
$G = \left(\frac{\sum x_i}{4}, \frac{\sum y_i}{4}, \frac{\sum z_i}{4}\right)$
